# Trade Match — Brand Kit

> Paste this into any Claude session before generating designs, slides, graphics, or copy for Trade Match. Everything should come out looking like one product.

## Identity in one sentence

Trade Match looks like a well-organized job site at dusk: dark, warm, amber worklight glow, zero fluff.

## Palette (use these exact values)

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#101215` | Page background |
| Panel | `#181b20` | Cards, surfaces |
| Panel 2 | `#1f2329` | Nested surfaces, chips, inputs |
| Line | `#2c3138` | Borders, dividers |
| Ink | `#eef1f4` | Primary text |
| Dim | `#9aa3ad` | Secondary text |
| **Amber** | `#f5a524` | THE accent. CTAs, highlights, progress |
| Amber bright | `#ffc257` | Gradient partner for amber |
| Green | `#4ec98a` | Positive callouts only ("why you matched") |
| Red | `#e8705a` | Warnings only ("before you commit") |

**Accent discipline:** amber is rationed. One amber moment per screen (the CTA, the top match, the progress). If everything glows, nothing glows. Green and red never decorate; they only mean good news and warnings.

## Typography

- System UI sans stack (no webfonts; product runs offline): `ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto`
- Headlines: tight letter-spacing (-0.02em to -0.03em), weight 700-800
- Body: 15-16px, line-height ~1.5
- Labels/eyebrows: 11-12.5px, uppercase, letter-spacing +0.07em to +0.09em, dim color
- Numbers (pay figures) are heroes: big, bold, ink or amber

## Logo / mark

Hammer-and-pick glyph (⚒) in a rounded amber gradient square, dark ink glyph. Wordmark: "Trade Match" bold, with the small dim tagline underneath: "Find the skilled trade that fits how you actually work."

## Voice rules

1. Blunt and warm, never corporate. "No right answers. Answer honestly, not how you think you should."
2. Numbers over adjectives. Say "$106,580 median," not "great pay."
3. Honest about downsides. Every trade gets a real warning. Never hide the hard parts.
4. Second person, present tense. "You're 80 feet up on a steel beam."
5. Short sentences. One idea each.
6. Never talk down to the reader. The audience includes laid-off engineers and working parents.
7. No em dashes. Commas, periods, or parentheses.

## Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Dark backgrounds, amber accent, generous padding | White backgrounds, blue "tech startup" gradients |
| Real BLS numbers with the source named | Rounded vague claims ("six figures easy!") |
| One accent moment per screen | Rainbow charts, multiple glow colors |
| Job-site metaphors (worklight, blueprint, toolbelt) | Robot/AI clip art, circuit-board patterns |
| "Built at the Claude Impact Lab, Chicago" credit | Any other logo lockups without asking Desi |

## Boilerplate blurbs (copy-paste)

**Short:** Trade Match is a 5-minute test that tells you which skilled trade fits how you actually work, backed by real BLS pay data.

**Medium:** AI is disrupting desk jobs. Trade Match maps you to the trades it can't touch: a 5-minute assessment, 16 trades ranked by fit with real government pay data, and next, a paid month with a working local tradesperson before you bet tuition on the switch.

**Event credit line:** Built with Claude at the Claude Impact Lab, Chicago.
